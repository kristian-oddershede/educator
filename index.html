Talks & Workshops

formidling så folk lytter. communication that connects.

Med seks års erfaring inden for naturvidenskabelig formidling gør jeg moderne fysik levende, let tilgængelig og inspirerende for mennesker i alle aldre. With six years of experience in science communication, I share stories about modern physics in an engaging and accessible way, inspiring curiosity in audiences of all ages.

Jeg afholder foredrag og workshops baseret på min erfaring med at træne forskere i formdiling ved CERN – den europæiske organisation bag verdens største videnskabelige eksperiment. I host talks and workshops based on my experience designing meaningful science outreach training for scientists at CERN – the European organization behind the world’s largest scientific experiment.

Sideløbende har jeg de seneste seks år faciliteret trivselsworkshops for unge gennem et unikt samtalespil, som jeg har designet med støtte fra Novo Nordisk Fonden. In parallel, I have spent the past six years facilitating wellbeing workshops for young people through a conversation game I designed with support from the Novo Nordisk Foundation.
